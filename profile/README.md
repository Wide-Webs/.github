## 🧠 About Wide Webs

**Wide Webs** is a technology and software engineering studio focused on the **conception, design, and development of digital products**.

We build **web platforms, SaaS, MicroSaaS, and custom software**, combining engineering, product thinking, user experience, and business vision.

> **Technology is the means. The product and the outcome are the goal.**

---

## ⚡ What We Do

- 🚀 **SaaS & MicroSaaS** — Digital products built to scale.
- 🌐 **Web Platforms** — Complete systems for businesses and operations.
- ⚙️ **Custom Software** — Solutions tailored to specific business needs.
- 🔌 **Integrations & APIs** — Connected systems and automated workflows.
- 🤖 **AI & Automation** — Intelligence applied to products and operations.
- 🎨 **UI/UX** — Fast, intuitive, and action-oriented interfaces.
